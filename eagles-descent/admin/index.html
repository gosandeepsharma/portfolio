What maps to what. A flight begun is sim_started, and its share of page views is the number that matters, because the sim needs a keyboard and phone traffic bounces. Outcomes split into sim_landed and sim_crashed by grade, with speeds, fuel and duration on each. The briefing is learn_opened, and learn_seen on a flight tells you whether that pilot read it first. The guided tutorial reports guide_step one through five.

Checking the pipe. Open the sim with ?analytics=debug and watch the browser console. That flag also overrides Do Not Track, which your own browser sends, so ordinary visits from you are never counted.
